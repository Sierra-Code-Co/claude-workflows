## What the number is not

30 is a count of **matched identifiers**, not of `console.*` calls: a call passing two
raw caught values counts twice. That is the right unit for a remediation checklist and
the wrong one for "how many log lines are affected". Recorded here because the two are
easy to conflate and the difference is invisible in the exit code.

## Provenance of the rule itself

The rule reproduces this result only because its own proof was fixed first. Moving it
out of `specs/` exposed that `ast-grep scan --rule <rule> <fixture>` returned **zero
findings and exit 0** against the fixture it was validated on — the fixture lived at a
path matching neither `files:` glob, so the scan was inert. Both halves of the check
(fixture set-equality, and the seeded-violation proof in
`tests/proofs/raw_caught_to_logger.sh`) now run at glob-matching paths. See
`tests/unit/test_structural_rules.py` for the full reasoning.
